About Me

Hello, my name is Fajar Adie Santosa, and I come from Yogyakarta, Indonesia. I am an ambitious learner with a strong passion for technology, especially in the fields of algorithms and programming. I also have a deep appreciation for numbers, which I see as a form of art.

My Journey

My journey started from curiosity — wondering how things work and why numbers make so much sense. That curiosity slowly pulled me into the world of algorithms and programming, where logic meets creativity. Along the way, I realized that doing everything alone isn't always the best way. I enjoy working with others — sharing ideas, solving problems together, and laughing through the chaos when things don't go as planned. Teamwork keeps things fun and meaningful. For me, every project is more than just getting things done — it's about the people I meet, the lessons I learn, and the little stories that happen in between.
